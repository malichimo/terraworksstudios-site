TerraWorks Studios, Inc. is a mobile app studio based in Mansfield, Massachusetts. Our app, TerraMine, turns the real world into a map of properties you can claim, mine, and share with friends.

Our app: TerraMine

TerraMine is a free mobile game for iOS and Android. Players claim real-world grid-square properties, play short mine-themed mini-games, and check into other players' mines to leave messages and photos. The game is supported by advertising.
